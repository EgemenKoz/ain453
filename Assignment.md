1 Introduction
The objective of this assignment is to familiarize you with the hardware and software
ecosystem of the Duckiebot. You are expected to perform system initialization, camera
calibration, and implement a basic vision-based control loop to ensure safe navigation.
2 Task Descriptions
2.1 Task 1: System Initialization & Configuration
Ensure that your Duckiebot is fully operational with all necessary ROS and Docker
environments ready for deployment.
2.2 Task 2: Camera Calibration
Perform camera calibration
2.3 Task 3: Navigation and Precision Braking Mission
In this task, first initialize your robot to move a straight line; as it progresses,
your system must continuously poll data from the Time-of-Flight (ToF) sensor
to monitor the environment. The mission concludes when the robot detects an
upcoming obstacle and executes a precise stop exactly 10 cm before contact.
2.4 Task 4: Real-time Visualization
While the robot is in motion, you must provide a live stream of the camera
feed on an external monitor.
The feed should verify that the robot is correctly ”perceiving” its environment.
